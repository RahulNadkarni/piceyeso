After you download

  1. Unzip the archive (or open the disk image).
  2. Open PicEyeSo. It is its own window, not a browser tab.
  3. Choose Open a scan or a folder. The file stays here.

Your file stays here

The app reads the scan on this computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Type ETDRS sector thicknesses and the macula updates as you type. Or open a real OCT volume.
